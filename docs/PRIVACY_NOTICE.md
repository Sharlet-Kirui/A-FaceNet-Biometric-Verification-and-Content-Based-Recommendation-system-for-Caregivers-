# CareMatch Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 · updated 12 November 2026

Shown in the mobile app before an account is created. The caregiver must accept it to register, and the API records the version accepted and the time (`caregivers.privacy_version`, `privacy_accepted_at`). Biometric processing and the registry check need separate, explicit consent at the document step.

## Who we are

CareMatch verifies the identity of home caregivers and matches them with suitable care jobs. It is run as a final-year research project at Strathmore University, Nairobi. The project team decides how your data is used and is responsible for protecting it under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. Contact us at carematch.project@gmail.com.

## What we collect

• Account: your name, email address, phone number and password (stored only in scrambled form).
• Personal details: date of birth, gender, nationality, ID or passport number, and address.
• Professional details: years of experience, Certificate of Good Conduct details, licences, work history, skills, languages and availability.
• Documents: photos of your ID or passport, your Certificate of Good Conduct, CV and any other documents you upload.
• Face data: your selfie, the head-turn photos, and the face measurements (face templates) worked out from them and from your ID photo.
• Results: verification results, your trust score, the jobs matched to you, and the jobs you express interest in.

## Why we use it

• To confirm that you are who you say you are, and to prevent fraud.
• To recommend jobs that fit your skills, location, experience and availability.
• To send you verification codes and tell you about decisions on your application and jobs.
• To keep a record of every decision, so it can be checked and corrected.
• To measure how well the system works, using results that do not identify you.

## Face data and your ID

Face data is sensitive personal data under the Act. We use it only to check that your selfie matches your ID photo and that a real person took it. We never use it for anything else, and we never sell it. Your ID number is also protected: it is stored encrypted. Before the selfie step we ask for your separate, explicit consent to these checks and to checking your details against the national identity register. You can refuse, but your identity cannot then be verified.

## Who can see it

• Authorised CareMatch administrators, who review applications and manage jobs.
• When you express interest in a job: the family or agency it belongs to, through our administrators, sees your name, county, experience, skills, trust badge, contact details and CV.
• The identity register check, which receives only your ID number, name and date of birth.
• Companies that host our servers and send our emails, only to provide those services.
We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## How we protect it

Connections are encrypted. Passwords are scrambled, identity numbers are encrypted, and only administrators with an account can open documents. Every administrator decision is recorded. If a data breach puts you at risk, we will tell you and the Data Commissioner without delay.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to close your account, we delete your documents, photos and face data within 30 days. We keep only the record of past verification decisions, which shows no images, for up to 12 months to prevent fraud, then delete it as well.

## Your rights

You have the right to be told how your data is used, to see the data we hold about you, to correct it (most details can be edited in your profile), to ask us to delete it, to object to its use, and to withdraw your consent. Email carematch.project@gmail.com to use these rights. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

## Age

CareMatch is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

## Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will show you the new version and ask you to accept it again.
